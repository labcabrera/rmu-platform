= ADR 0005: Sense talents and perception by sense

* *Status:* Accepted (2026-11-04, workspace spec 076)
* *Date:* 2026-10-20
* *Context spec:* workspace spec 008, talents (`specs/008-talents/` in the workspace meta-repo)
* *Decided in:* workspace spec 076, development, combat talents and senses (`specs/076-development-combat-talents-senses/`, block C)

== Context

Core Law chapter 4 has many talents and flaws whose effect is a bonus or penalty to Perception, but only when the Perception roll uses one specific sense. In the catalog these are:

* acute and poor hearing, smell, taste, touch and vision;
* the racial hearing (cat, dog, hare) and sight (eagle, gecko, hawk) talents;
* Heatsense.

Others change what a character can perceive at all, or the penalties for light and darkness:

* Nightvision, Darkvision, Light Sensitivity, Tetrachromatic Vision and Third Eyelid;
* Missing Sense;
* Extra Sense. From tier III, Extra Sense also allows attacking unseen targets.

The engine has no notion of senses:

* `rmu-api-strategic` has a single `perception` skill (with specializations) and an armor `perceptionPenalty`.
* `rmu-api-tactical` resolves Perception as an absolute maneuver. Its only situational modifier is light.
* Combat has no visibility or lighting model.

Spec 008 decided to ignore these talents until perception by sense exists. Adding their bonus to the whole Perception skill would apply it to every roll, which is wrong for most of them.

== Questions to decide

. *Where does the sense live?* Options:
.. a `sense` field on the Perception maneuver request in tactical (`hearing`, `smell`, `taste`, `touch`, `vision`, plus `extra` for Extra Sense), so that sense talents become situational modifiers of that maneuver;
.. Perception specializations in strategic, one per sense, each with its own bonus. This changes the skill model and every existing character.
. *How are the talents described?* As entries of the strategic trait effect registry with a `sense` condition, exported to tactical as data, or as one tactical modifier per talent.
. *Light and darkness:* Nightvision, Darkvision and Light Sensitivity need a light level in the combat situation, as well as in maneuvers, to change attack and maneuver penalties. Does it belong to this decision, or to a separate one about lighting?
. *Missing Sense:* the book leaves the penalty (from -20 to -50) to the game master. Is it a fixed default, or always declared?

== Decision

. *Where the sense lives:* option 1.1. The Perception and Tracking maneuvers in `rmu-api-tactical` take a `sense` (`vision`, `hearing`, `smell`, `taste`, `touch`, `extra`) and a `senseUsage` (`required` or `helpful`). The defaults are `vision` and `required`. The strategic skill model does not change.
. *How the talents are described:* as tactical modifiers computed from the actor's traits by one pure function (`shared/domain/senses/sense-modifiers.ts`). They appear in a `senses` group of the maneuver listing, each with its rule reference. Oblivious and Direction Sense stay as fixed effects in the strategic sheet.
. *Light and darkness are part of this decision:*
.. light applies to Perception only when the sense is vision (`required`), and to Tracking as `helpful`;
.. the light table carries the Nightvision column, Darkvision cancels the light penalty within its range (declared on the roll), and Light Sensitivity penalizes bright light;
.. the tactical game has an ambient light level (`GameEnvironment.light`), used as the default of every maneuver and movement; the request may override it. The strategic game has no light level.
. *Missing Sense:* -100 when the sense is required. When it only helps, -20 by default, editable down to -50 on the roll.

In combat, only two rules are added: Extra Sense tier III-V reduces the penalty against unseen targets to -50, -25 and 0, and Light Sensitivity penalizes OB under bright ambient light. Light as a general attack and defense modifier is left to a separate ADR on lighting in combat.

NPC sense talents are free-text names in `rmu-api-npcs`. They are translated to catalog trait ids (`NpcTalent.traitId`, migration 077) and copied to the tactical actor round like character traits.

== Consequences

* Sense talents have no effect on the strategic sheet: a Perception total sent to tactical does not include them.
* `rmu-mfe-tactical` needs a sense picker, the sense selections and the ambient light control (spec 076, phase 5).
* NPC talents without a catalog id (Tremorsense, lists of several missing senses) have no effect.
* A later ADR decides lighting in combat (attack and defense modifiers, light sources and distances).
