= ADR 0004: How the shield maneuver enters tactical combat

* *Status:* Accepted (2026-11-05, option 1.1)
* *Date:* 2026-10-19
* *Context spec:* workspace spec 007, combat rules alignment (`specs/007-combat-rules-alignment/` in the workspace meta-repo); decided in workspace spec 077, block A1 (`specs/077-core-law-high-priority-gaps/`)
* *Related:* link:0002-stun-effect.adoc[ADR 0002], link:0003-effect-duration-rounds.adoc[ADR 0003]

== Context

Under Core Law §9.6 (Shields, Table 9-6), a shield covers a limited number of attacks per round. The first covered attack gets the shield's DB automatically. Each further attack the defender wants to cover needs a successful Shield maneuver, made by the defender.

After spec 007, `rmu-api-tactical`:

* counts the attacks the shield has covered in the round (`currentBlocks`) and stops adding the shield's DB once `blockCount` is reached;
* ignores the shield against flank and rear attacks;
* adds passive, partial and full blocking from the defender's Shield skill.

It does not ask for the maneuver. Until this ADR is decided, the shield covers its attacks up to `blockCount` with no roll.

The attack flow is driven by the attacker's player or the game master: declare, prepare (modifiers), attack roll, critical and fumble rolls, apply. The defender takes no part in it, apart from the parry declared beforehand.

== Questions to decide

. *Who rolls?* Options:
.. the defender's player, in a new step of the attacker's resolution (`pending_shield_roll`), before the attack roll;
.. the game master, with a "shield maneuver passed" switch in the attack modifiers;
.. nobody: the maneuver is ignored, and `blockCount` alone limits the shield.
. *What difficulty and what result?* The maneuver is absolute. Is the result read as pass/fail only, or do partial successes give part of the DB?
. *When does it count?* Is it rolled for each attack after the first, or once for all the attacks of the same attacker?
. *How does it interact with full and partial blocking?* Do they replace the maneuver or add to it?

== Decision

Option 1.1, inside the existing parry step of the attack flow (workspace spec 077, decisions A1.1-A1.8):

. *Who rolls:* the defender's player or the game master, in the parry step (`PATCH /actions/:id/attack/parry`). An action whose attacks need a Shield maneuver goes through the `parry` status even when nobody parries, and stays there until every maneuver is rolled or decided.
. *When it is asked:* when an attack is prepared and the shield already used or reserved at least one block in the round, the attack gets `shieldManeuver: {targetId, required: true, skill, modifiers}`. The shield and blocking DB are kept aside in `shieldManeuver.modifiers` and the `shield` plugin records the skipped rule `shield-maneuver-pending`. The block is reserved anyway: a failed maneuver also spends it.
. *Roll and result:* `shieldManeuvers: [{attackName, roll, difficulty?}]` in the parry body. It is an absolute maneuver: roll + the defender's Shield skill + the defender's penalties + difficulty (medium by default), read on the `generic` table. A success or an absolute success adds the shield and blocking DB; any other result records the skipped rule `shield-maneuver-failed`. There is no partial DB.
. *Game master shortcut:* `{attackName, passed: true | false}` with no roll, recorded as `manual`.
. *One roll per attack*, not per attacker.
. *Blocking does not replace the maneuver:* when it fails, neither the shield DB nor the passive, partial or full blocking count.
. *Errors:* `err-shield-maneuver-required` (rolling the attack while a maneuver is pending) and `err-shield-maneuver-not-needed` (a maneuver sent for an attack that has none).
. *Steady Shield* (fighting style ability): each time it is taken adds one block to `blockCount` when the actor round is built.

== Consequences

* `rmu-api-tactical` asks for and resolves the maneuver: `shieldManeuver` on the attack, `shieldManeuvers` in the parry body, the two skipped rules and the two errors above.
* `rmu-mfe-tactical` needs the maneuver rows next to the parries in the parry step, with the game master shortcut.
* The difficulty is not given by the rules: medium is the default and the game master can change it per attack.
